A few things you

Something else on your mind?
We’re always happy to chat.

Can you help with just part of our event?+

Absolutely. We offer full planning, partial planning and on-the-day coordination. Tell us where you are in the process and we’ll find the right level of support.

How far ahead should we get in touch?+

We recommend 6–12 months for weddings and larger events. If your date is closer, get in touch to discuss availability.

Do you plan destination events?+

Yes. We love creating experiences in unique locations, both locally and internationally. Share the place you have in mind and we’ll talk through the possibilities.

Does everything have to be luxury?+

Not at all. While we specialise in elegant, high-end experiences, we tailor each event to your style and budget, always with a refined and personal touch.
